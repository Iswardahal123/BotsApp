## 2026-06-09 - Alphabetical Sorting and Command Count in Help Menu
**Learning:** In chat-based interfaces, users rely heavily on visual scanning to find commands. Alphabetical sorting significantly reduces the cognitive load required to find a specific command. Providing a total count gives immediate feedback on the bot's capabilities and scale.
**Action:** Always sort dynamic lists alphabetically by default and provide summary statistics (like total counts) for large collections.
