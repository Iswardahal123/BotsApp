## 2025-05-22 - [Scannability and feedback]
**Learning:** Alphabetical sorting of command lists significantly improves scannability in chat-based interfaces where visual search is the primary mode of navigation. Providing a total command count in the header offers immediate feedback on the bot's scale. Visual feedback (like reactions) should only be triggered after confirming the action is valid to avoid misleading the user.
**Action:** Always sort dynamic lists presented to users and ensure feedback is tied to successful validation.
