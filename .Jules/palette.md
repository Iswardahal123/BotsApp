## 2025-05-14 - Alphabetical Sorting and Scannability in Chat Interfaces
**Learning:** In chat-based interfaces where users scroll through long lists (like a help menu), alphabetical sorting significantly reduces cognitive load and improves scannability compared to arbitrary loading order. Providing a total count also gives immediate feedback on the bot's capabilities.
**Action:** Always sort command lists alphabetically in bot interfaces and include summary metadata (like total count) to orient the user.
