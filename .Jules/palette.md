## 2026-06-05 - [Enhanced Help Menu Scannability]
**Learning:** In chat-based interfaces, scannability is paramount as users often have to scroll through long lists. Alphabetical sorting significantly reduces cognitive load during visual searches. Additionally, providing metadata like a 'Total Commands' count gives immediate feedback on the application's scale and completeness.
**Action:** Always sort lists alphabetically in chat interfaces and provide summary metadata to help users orient themselves.
