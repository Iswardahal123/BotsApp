## 2025-05-14 - [Help Menu Scannability and Command Feedback]
**Learning:** Alphabetical sorting of commands and providing a total count in the header significantly improves navigation and scannability in chat interfaces. Visual feedback (like reactions) should only be triggered after verifying the command exists to prevent misleading users on invalid input.
**Action:** Always sort dynamic lists of options alphabetically and provide summary statistics (like counts) in menus. Ensure success indicators are tied to actual success/validity.
