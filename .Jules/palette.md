## 2025-05-15 - Alphabetical Command Sorting and Visual Scale
**Learning:** In chat-based interfaces where users scroll through long lists of commands, alphabetical sorting significantly reduces cognitive load during visual search. Providing a total command count in the header offers immediate feedback on the bot's scale and helps users verify they are seeing the full list.
**Action:** Always sort command lists alphabetically and include a 'Total' metric in help menus or catalogs to improve scannability and provide context.
