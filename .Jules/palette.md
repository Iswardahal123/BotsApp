## 2025-05-14 - [Alphabetical Sorting and Command Count in Help Menu]
**Learning:** Alphabetical sorting of long lists in chat interfaces significantly reduces cognitive load during visual search. Providing a "Total Commands" count gives users immediate feedback on the bot's capabilities and helps them understand the scale of the system.
**Action:** Always ensure lists of commands or options are sorted alphabetically by default and include summary metadata (like counts) in headers to provide context.
