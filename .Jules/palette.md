## 2025-05-15 - [Alphabetical Sorting for Scannability]
**Learning:** In chat-based interfaces where users interact with command lists, alphabetical sorting significantly improves scannability. Without sorting, users must read every entry to find what they need; with sorting, they can quickly navigate to the desired letter.
**Action:** Always ensure command lists or options are sorted logically (usually alphabetically) when presented to the user.

## 2025-05-15 - [Accurate Status Reporting]
**Learning:** Users rely on status messages (e.g., "Enabled: False") to understand the bot's state. Providing incorrect feedback (copy-paste errors) leads to confusion and perceived unreliability.
**Action:** Double-check response templates for boolean states and ensure they accurately reflect the underlying logic.
